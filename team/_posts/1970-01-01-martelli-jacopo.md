Jacopo Martelli is a summer software intern in the Getz Lab, where he works on Ollie, an internal AI laboratory assistant for the Cancer Genome Analysis group. He joins from Rome, where he studies Latin, Ancient Greek, and philosophy at the Liceo Classico, which he claims is surprisingly good preparation for debugging Python.

Outside the Broad, Jacopo founded Luxemia, a federated AI system for predicting leukaemia relapse, and will represent Italy at EUCYS 2026. He is also COO of AgeSync and works on research in computational oncology. His networking strategy is simple: email interesting people until someone says yes.

Outside the lab, Jacopo trains MMA, cooks aggressively Italian food, and looks after two English bulldogs, Nina and Sugar, neither of whom is particularly impressed by his research.
