Kiran is a PhD student in the Bioinformatics and Integrative Genomics program at Harvard Medical School. He received his Bachelor of Science in Engineering in Operations Research and Financial Engineering from Princeton University in 2025.

In the Getz Lab, Kiran develops and applies computational methods to uncover the genetic drivers and structural determinants of tumors, with an emphasis on identifying therapeutic targets and understanding treatment resistance. In his free time, Kiran enjoys cooking, playing soccer, and hiking.
